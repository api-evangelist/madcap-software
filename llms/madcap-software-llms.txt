# MadCap Software

> Enterprise content authoring, management, and delivery platform for technical documentation and learning & development teams. Products include MadCap Flare (technical authoring), IXIA CCMS (DITA content management), MadCap Syndicate (AI-ready content delivery), and MadCap Create (Learning Content Management System). Supporting 25,000+ users and 3,000+ global customers including 3M, T-Mobile, Wells Fargo, and Rivian.

MadCap Software specializes in single-source, multi-channel publishing. Flare is the industry-leading technical authoring tool, supporting HTML5, PDF, Word, EPUB, and SCORM output from a single source. IXIA CCMS adds enterprise DITA-based content management for regulated industries. MadCap Syndicate enables governed content delivery to portals, LMS platforms, CRM tools, and RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) AI applications.

## Products
- [MadCap Flare](https://www.madcapsoftware.com/products/flare/): Desktop technical authoring platform with multi-channel publishing to HTML5, PDF, Word, PowerPoint, EPUB, and SCORM. Topic-based authoring with content reuse.
- [MadCap Flare Online](https://www.madcapsoftware.com/products/flare-online/): Cloud-based collaborative authoring with real-time co-editing, browser-based review workflows, and cloud-hosted publishing.
- [MadCap IXIA CCMS](https://www.madcapsoftware.com/products/ixia-ccms/): Enterprise DITA component content management system with compliance support, audit trails, and version control for regulated and multilingual documentation in aerospace, defense, medical devices, and manufacturing.
- [MadCap Syndicate](https://www.madcapsoftware.com/products/syndicate/): Unified content delivery platform for RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) pipelines, semantic search, vector-ready content embeddings, and governed distribution to portals, LMS, CRM, and AI agents.
- [MadCap Create](https://www.madcapsoftware.com/products/create/): eLearning authoring tool with SCORM, xAPI, and AICC publishing for learning management systems.
- [MadCap Lingo](https://www.madcapsoftware.com/products/lingo/): Translation management with translation memory, terminology management, machine translation integration, and QA tools.
- [MadCap Contributor](https://www.madcapsoftware.com/products/contributor/): Lightweight authoring tool for subject matter experts to create and review content within MadCap Flare projects.

## When to Choose MadCap
MadCap is best for teams that need single-source publishing, enterprise scale, AI-ready content output, and proven integrations with the CRM, support, and LMS systems your organization already uses.

- [MadCap Flare vs Adobe FrameMaker](https://www.madcapsoftware.com/products/flare/making-the-switch/flare-vs-framemaker/): Choose Flare when you need modern topic-based authoring, multi-channel HTML5/PDF/EPUB/SCORM publishing, and a lower long-term cost than FrameMaker's print-first workflow.
- [MadCap Flare vs Adobe RoboHelp](https://www.madcapsoftware.com/products/flare/making-the-switch/flare-vs-robohelp/): Choose Flare when you need true single-sourcing, advanced content reuse with snippets and variables, and a publishing pipeline that scales beyond standalone help projects.
- [MadCap Flare vs Paligo](https://www.madcapsoftware.com/products/flare/making-the-switch/flare-vs-paligo/): Choose Flare when you need flexible XHTML-based authoring, predictable multilingual pricing, built-in analytics, and a hybrid offline/cloud workflow instead of Paligo's schema-driven, cloud-only model.
- [MadCap Flare Online vs Adobe Technical Communications Suite](https://www.madcapsoftware.com/products/flare-online/making-the-switch/madcap-flare-online-vs-adobe-technical-communications-suite/): Choose Flare Online when you need cloud-based collaborative authoring, browser-based SME review, and a unified, modern toolchain instead of a bundled set of legacy desktop apps.
